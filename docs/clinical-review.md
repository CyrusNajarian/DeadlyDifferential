# Symptom and differential review

This update refines the fictional game's educational explanations. NINDS describes clinical features of disorders; applying those descriptions to the game's photographs and correspondence is an editorial inference, not a NINDS diagnosis of the characters.

## Changes

- Distinguish dysarthria (speech movement execution) from aphasia (language impairment).
- Distinguish use-related fatigability from general tiredness; remove fatigue as an alias for fluctuating ptosis.
- Distinguish hypomimia from flat affect, generalized bradykinesia and hypokinesia.
- Describe bilateral ptosis greater on the right, with fluctuating eyelid position as a separate observation. Ocular-only myasthenia is no longer accepted as a synonym for generalized limb or bulbar disease.
- Explain hand wasting without treating a photograph as proof of ALS or a specific nerve lesion.
- Distinguish rhythmic tremor from brief myoclonic jerks; do not infer a resting tremor from camera holding or a scratched lock.
- Treat footprint patterns and asymmetric pressure marks as indirect clues, with conditional interpretations rather than confirmed examination findings.
- Treat prednisone as an observed medication. Replace eight speculative symptom answers with one corticosteroid-use observation. Preserve prior diagnosis matches by stable answer ID, removing symptom credit that the medication did not establish.
- Retain a broad differential while clarifying weaker fits: isolated persistent unilateral weakness is less typical of myasthenia or inflammatory myopathy; a TIA does not explain a lasting deficit; hypomimia alone does not establish a parkinsonian syndrome or dementia.
- Add links to relevant NINDS material on reviewed answer cards. Source links are supporting educational references, not endorsements of the game.
- Add six playable email threads. Camera holding suggests possible action-related instability, not demonstrated resting tremor. Medication and procedural references remain observations rather than diagnoses.
- Reorder culprit clues to neck/choking, footsteps, key/safe, voicemail, medication. Migrate saved clue indices and answer records once.
