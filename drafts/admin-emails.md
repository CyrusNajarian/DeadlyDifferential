## Organizer notes — exclude from player-facing emails

- Silver: navigation imaging before tissue sampling suggests investigation of a focal lesion. Alongside the headache belongings it supports an intracranial mass more than an uncomplicated migraine, but does not establish a tumor or malignancy. Tissue sampling can have other indications.
- Emerald: abrupt admission and phased recovery support a vascular history; incomplete equivalent fields reinforce the separate spatial-attention clue. Neither detail establishes stroke alone.
- Charcoal: camera instability is a nonspecific observation. It does not prove a resting tremor or Parkinson disease.
- Ivory: loss of body bulk plus hand difficulty contributes to a motor pattern. It is not specific to ALS.
- Navy: decline with repeated effort and improvement after rest is the intended fatigability clue.
- Rust: sun-related skin symptoms support photosensitivity, with several possible causes.
- For a subtler Silver version, remove “navigation” and retain only the scan, procedure, and sample. For a more specific version, change “sample” to “tissue sample.”
